## 2. Gate Review Summary & Justification

The CivicConnect engineering team has completed all required Milestone 1 foundation deliverables. The team has demonstrated:
1. **Clear Engineering Intent:** Moving beyond superficial coding to define an auditable, traceable, and defensible requirements baseline.
2. **Strict Boundary Management:** Deliberately deferring final software architecture and technology-stack decisions to Milestone 2, preserving options without premature lock-in.
3. **Rigorous Governance:** Operationalizing the protected-main branch policy with mandatory two-reviewer approval across all substantive contributions.
4. **Responsible AI Accountability:** Logging all AI-assisted research and verifying outputs against domain standards.

The engineering baseline `PED v1.0` provides a robust, controlled foundation to proceed directly to **Milestone 2 (Architecture, Design & Engineering Decisions)**.
